# Refusals and feedback

News lands where it happened and lasts as long as its cause: inline for what the person is
touching, a banner for a lasting state of a pane, a toast for an event off-screen or asked from no
surface, a pill for one thing's state. A refusal says why, in words, where the change was asked.
The channel table is
[ux-rules: feedback](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/ux-rules.md#feedback) (U44); where each refusal lands is
[patterns: refusals](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#refusals).
