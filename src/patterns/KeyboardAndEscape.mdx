# Keyboard and Escape

Every action has a keyboard path; every app binds the standard keys (Mod+K for the palette, `?`
for the shortcut sheet, Delete, F, A, and Mod+S and Mod+Z where its model has them); Escape closes
the innermost thing first. The rules are in
[ux-rules: focus and keyboard](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/ux-rules.md#focus-and-keyboard) and
[ux-rules: Escape](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/ux-rules.md#escape).

## Escape, innermost first

A focused toast, then the palette, a dialog or the shortcut sheet, then the inspector's sheet while
the focus is in it, then the app's own Escape. Two stories play it with the keyboard; open each on
its own, as it needs the focus of the whole frame:
[AppShell's Escape order](?path=/story/shell-appshell--escape-order) (a dialog takes Escape before
the inspector's sheet, at the narrow width) and
[Toaster's Escape](?path=/story/composites-toaster--escape) (a focused toast goes first).
