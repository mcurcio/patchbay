# Connecting

A drag from a handle; every candidate port says yes or no from the app's one rule; a drop makes a
request (a ghost) or a queued change (a dashed wire); the source's answer makes it live or
refused. From the keyboard, each port in the inspector has a "Connect to" choice. The steps are in
[patterns: connecting](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#connecting).
