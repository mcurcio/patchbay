# Adding a node

The person asks (a double-click or a dropped wire on empty canvas, the palette, `A`), picks a type
in the palette opened at the point, configures it in the inspector, and adds it; the node appears
when the source holds it, at the point it was asked. The steps, states and do's are in
[patterns: adding a node](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#adding-a-node); where it goes after that, in
[placing nodes](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#placing-nodes).

## Asking at a point

Double-click the empty canvas, or drag a wire from `out` and drop it on empty canvas.
`useAddAt` gives the point (and the port the wire came from); here the story adds at once, where
