# Read-only, empty and offline

A read-only graph turns off every edit with the switches there are and says why in a `quiet`
banner, while reading, selecting and inspecting stay; an empty graph says how to add; a lost
connection says whether it is coming back, and the canvas keeps the last graph it had. The
switches and states are in [patterns: a read-only graph](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#a-read-only-graph) and
[patterns: empty, loading and offline](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#empty-loading-and-offline).
