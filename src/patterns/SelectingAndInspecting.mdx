# Selecting and inspecting

The inspector shows one subject, chosen by the selection: nothing selected is the graph, one node
is the node, one connection is the connection, several is the selection. Names and values are
edited there, never on the canvas. The table of subjects is in
[patterns: selecting and inspecting](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#selecting-and-inspecting).

## The selection chooses the subject

Select a node, the wire, or several (shift-click, or drag a box with shift held); click the empty
canvas to select nothing. A connection's subject names its two ends, each a button that selects it.
