# Touch and density

One touch query: under it every target grows to a full touch target, a tool group's a little more,
and text fields take their touch text size; handles and small marks grow their hit area, not
their drawing. The rules are in
[foundations: density and touch](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/foundations.md#density-and-touch).
To see the touch sizes, open this page in your browser's device emulation with touch on; this
page cannot switch the pointer for you.
