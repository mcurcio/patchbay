# Tones

A tone says what a state is, and an app maps each of its states to one of patchbay's seven state
tones and four change tones. What each means, and the rules F6 to F13, are in
[foundations: tones](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/foundations.md#tones).
Switch the brand and theme in the toolbar to see each one hold.
