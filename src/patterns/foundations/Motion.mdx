# Motion

Five durations, each for one role; entrances move and exits do not; the graph itself never
animates. The rules are in
[foundations: motion](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/foundations.md#motion),
and what changes under reduced motion in
[ux-rules: reduced motion](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/ux-rules.md#reduced-motion).
To see the reduced result, turn on your browser's or system's reduced-motion setting (in
Chromium's developer tools: Rendering, "Emulate CSS media feature prefers-reduced-motion"); this
page cannot switch it for you.
