# Live, pending, queued, refused

What the canvas shows is what the source holds; what the person asked for is drawn apart from it
until the source answers. **Pending** is a request sent and not answered yet; **queued** is a
change held until a save. An app applies each change at once or queues it, and both draw with the
same states: [patterns: two commit models](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#two-commit-models), and the rules in
[ux-rules: live, pending, queued, refused](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/ux-rules.md#live-pending-queued-refused).

## One request, stepped through

Connect sends the request: the button is busy and a ghost stands for the wire. Then answer for the
source. Accepted, the ghost becomes a live wire; refused, it turns `refused` and the reason is in
words. In an app the reason goes in a toast
([Feedback](?path=/docs/patterns-refusals-and-feedback--docs)).
