# Following the view

The view fits the whole graph whenever it changes shape or the canvas changes size, until the
person moves the view; only Fit follows again. Going to a node selects it, moves the view to it and
stops following. The rules are in [patterns: following the view](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#following-the-view)
and [going to a node](https://github.com/mcurcio/patchbay/blob/main/docs/patterns.md#going-to-a-node).

## Following, and stopping

Press "Add a node" and the view follows; pan or wheel and it stops; the fit tool follows again.

<Canvas of={FollowFitStories.All} />
